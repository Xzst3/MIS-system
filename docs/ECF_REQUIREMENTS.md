# ECF connected workspace — agreed scope

Updated 6 November 2026. The group’s ER diagram and dictionary have now been reviewed; see `ERD_ALIGNMENT.md` for the field-by-field coverage and explicit gaps.

## Source and scope

The stakeholder meeting and handout establish a beneficiary-centred information, referral and practical coordination foundation. The user then selected a simplified giving experience, email-based appointment requests, independent financial approvals, transparent resource use and shared identities across employee, volunteer and donor roles. The supplied enterprise/SAP requirements are integration references, not a mandate to implement SAP or every referenced module.

The latest giving decision supersedes the QR/third-party-only proposal: the public website now runs an interactive checkout with a processing state and completion reference. It charges no money. Its source is retained as `website_preview`, its external settlement flag remains false, and a financial reviewer must explicitly post it to the prototype ledger. No pledge is created.

## Business rules implemented

- Amounts are stored as integer USD cents. Other currencies are explicitly rejected; there is no implicit exchange conversion.
- Every entry has an explicit fund. General support must be allocated to a service or operations fund before expenditure.
- The reviewer cannot be the submitting account. A reviewer also cannot review a contribution or service linked to their own person profile.
- The finance officer's initial review authority is $2,500. Administrators may change this in Approval rules; the change is audited. This is a provisional starter setting, not a policy claimed to have been approved by ECF.
- Larger requests route to an administrator. Budget revisions themselves use independent approval.
- Approval is serialized in one database transaction. Balance, restrictions, budget, inventory and source-lot checks occur together with posting and audit creation. Repeated approval does not post twice.
- Contribution value follows source lots. Available sources are consumed in posting order, limited to stock/funds available on the transaction date. Transfers preserve value; expenditure consumes it. A shared cost retains its actual purpose rather than receiving a made-up beneficiary.
- Original receipt references are unique for cash/asset-sale receipts; invoice references are unique for expenses. A changed payload cannot reuse a completed submission's key.
- Approved service links cannot be moved to a different beneficiary/programme. Service dates can be rescheduled before hours are attached; dated hours preserve their service date.
- Completed service and distinct-beneficiary measures exclude planned and cancelled services. A beneficiary may appear in several programme counts; the overall distinct count is not their sum.
- Cash, annual spending, estimated in-kind value, people, completed services and volunteer hours remain separate measures. A service outcome is staff-entered evidence, not proof of a clinical improvement.
- Login permissions remain functional account permissions. An engagement is a business relationship with its own status, dates and availability. Volunteering does not enroll someone in payroll or automatically switch their permissions at weekends.
- New workforce engagements begin Pending. Active service eligibility requires the three verification flags. Evidence is referenced in notes; this release does not store screening-document uploads.

## Dashboard questions addressed

1. Where did a contribution go? Trace its original source, fund transfers, remaining amount and expense/service links.
2. What is available, restricted, spent or awaiting approval? Open fund reconciliations, annual budgets and the approval queue.
3. What happened to a physical gift? Inspect its source, stock history, distribution or conversion into sale proceeds.
4. Who received what support and when? Open the beneficiary's service journey with programme, provider, date, status and outcome.
5. How is activity changing? Compare the six-month service chart, dated records, programme measures and returning-donor definition.
6. How do overlapping roles fit together? Inspect one profile's engagements, weekday availability, linked account, separate hours and giving history.

## Provisional boundaries and ER follow-up

- Real card processing, provider settlement feeds, bank imports and real payment execution are not connected. Manual receipts require evidence confirmation; website-preview entries remain identifiable as previews in their source metadata.
- Approvals record authorization/accounting; they do not send a bank payment. A production payment authorization/execution/reconciliation model must be agreed separately.
- This release does not implement SAP, payroll, supplier procurement/PO matching, international FX, automated campaign messaging or a complete statutory accounting system.
- Sample budgets, qualification flags and example business figures are fictional bootstrap data for the user-requested preview. They must not be treated as real ECF financial or personnel evidence.
- Email is currently a unique contact matching key. Shared household/organization email cases and full reviewed profile merging need a policy and the forthcoming ER model. Person UUIDs and all transaction references should be preserved during that migration.
- The initial fund list consists of general support, seven service designations and operations. Programmes, delegated limits, annual budgets, outcome definitions, evidence retention and document handling remain subject to ECF validation.
- Older finance/intake list views load the latest 1,000 financial requests, 3,000 posting lines and 300 intake receipts. The new Reports & impact queries all matching rows for its metrics, paginates 25 rows per page, and exports up to 20,000 matches with an explicit size error beyond that limit. The funding trace now computes complete source totals separately and paginates ledger details.
- No pledge workflow is used by the public checkout or new dashboard. Historical legacy records are retained rather than silently converted into received money.
- The old `/demo` entry redirects to the authenticated `/workspace` so users see the current connected experience.

## Validation

`npm test` checks role boundaries. `scripts/integration-test.mjs` checks authentication, legacy record permissions, public intake and account lifecycle. `scripts/business-integration-test.mjs` checks the connected financial/service/person workflows against the configured Neon database through a localhost server, including concurrency and exact scoped fixture cleanup. Source types and production compilation are checked separately.
