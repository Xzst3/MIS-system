# ECF ER diagram alignment with the connected workspace

Reviewed 6 November 2026 against the user-supplied draw.io source, diagram image, Oracle DDL/DML and spreadsheet dictionary. The diagram has **17 business entities**; the page's older “20 Tables” title is not the count of actual entities. The supplied DDL also defines 17 tables and 14 foreign keys. Course labels have no effect on application scope.

The live database is Neon PostgreSQL. The Oracle scripts are not executed, nor is their independent sample-data set imported over the existing linked records. Oracle NUMBER identities, VARCHAR2, NVL and PL/SQL require conversion. Existing UUIDs, user accounts, approvals, fund balances and beneficiary links are preserved.

This mapping is intentionally explicit about coverage. It is not a claim that the live database is a one-for-one implementation of every field in the supplied diagram.

## Business relationships retained beyond the supplied diagram

The stakeholder handout requires beneficiary-centred support history, programmes, funds/restrictions, allocations, outcomes and traceability. Those concepts are not fully modelled in the 17-entity diagram, so removing the live `service_records`, `funds`, `finance_lots`, `finance_postings`, `fund_budgets` or approvals would lose confirmed requirements. They remain explicit operational extensions.

The diagram's `GIFT_DISTRIBUTION.Recipient_DonorID` points to DONOR. Operational service recipients are not forced to donate or receive a donor engagement. A beneficiary links through the shared identity and service record instead. A person may separately become a donor later without changing their past service identity.

## Field/meaning decisions

- Donation valuation and cash receipt are separate. Derived `SUM(DonationLine.RecordedValue)` would describe the mixed receipt's total valuation, not money available to spend. Subsequent asset sale is a conversion, not a second donation.
- No Large/Small donation threshold was approved. Reports retain exact amounts and do not fabricate classifications.
- Actual repeat gifts can be counted by date. No pledge workflow or assumed receivable is reintroduced; the user explicitly chose direct giving.
- `FREE` and `IN_EXCHANGE`, tax eligibility and receipt issuance need validated rules. Those columns are not inferred from free text.
- Original person/source/transaction UUIDs are stable across the current extension. The only new write attributes in this reporting change are expense classification and its audit; the provenance view reads existing financial facts.
- Grant receipt, other-income intake, mixed-line gifts, exact shifts and statutory receipts remain visible design gaps for a later agreed workflow, rather than silently changing the meaning of existing records.
